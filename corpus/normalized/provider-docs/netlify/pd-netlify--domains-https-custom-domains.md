Registering a domain means purchasing the rights to use a specific domain name (like example.com) for a set period, typically one year.  When you register a domain, you're essentially renting that web address from a domain registrar, which is a company authorized to sell domain registrations. A company can be a domain registrar and offer DNS (Domain Name System) services to help site visitors look up your domain name and to help you manage your domain. This gives you exclusive rights to use that domain name for your website or email.      How do I know if I've registered a domain?   If you've registered a domain, you should have received confirmation emails from the registrar and billing records for the domain purchase.  You can also check domain ownership using WHOIS lookup tools online, such as  lookup.icann.org/en . You can also check your email for receipts from your domain registrar.
