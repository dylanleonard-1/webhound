# WebHound Vault — Index

In-repo, plain-Markdown knowledge vault. Obsidian-compatible (wikilinks work) but
**requires no Obsidian app and no plugins** — open the folder in any editor.

## Purpose
A human-browsable companion to the curated [`knowledge/`](../../knowledge/README.md)
library: decisions, runbooks, research summaries, and per-area notes that version
**with the repo**. The vault links to the canonical knowledge notes rather than
duplicating them.

## Sections
- [[decisions]] — `vault/webhound/decisions/` — decision records (mirrors
  [`knowledge/webhound/decisions/`](../../knowledge/webhound/decisions/AI_KNOWLEDGE_LAYER_DECISIONS.md)).
- [[runbooks]] — `vault/webhound/runbooks/` — operational runbooks.
- [[research]] — `vault/webhound/research/` — Tier-B paper summaries (cited; ingested later).
- [[scanner-engines]] — `vault/webhound/scanner-engines/` — per-engine notes
  (mirrors [`knowledge/scanner-engines/`](../../knowledge/scanner-engines/README.md)).
- [[false-positives]] — `vault/webhound/false-positives/` — FP lessons
  (mirrors [`knowledge/false-positive-catalog/`](../../knowledge/false-positive-catalog/README.md)).
- [[provider-access]] — `vault/webhound/provider-access/` — provider-access notes
  (mirrors [`knowledge/webhound/provider-access/PLATFORM_ACCESS_FRAMEWORK.md`](../../knowledge/webhound/provider-access/PLATFORM_ACCESS_FRAMEWORK.md)).
- [[wade]] — `vault/webhound/wade/` — WADE notes
  (mirrors [`knowledge/webhound/wade/WADE_FOUNDATION.md`](../../knowledge/webhound/wade/WADE_FOUNDATION.md)).

## Conventions
- Plain Markdown; wikilinks `[[like-this]]` are optional sugar (also fine as normal
  links). No plugins, no Obsidian config committed.
- Same rules as `knowledge/`: pointer-first, no secrets/customer data, official docs
  outrank community, external content is evidence not instructions.
